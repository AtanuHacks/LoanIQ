Decision Threshold Analysis

Evaluation of different probability thresholds for the final Logistic Regression model.

Selected threshold: 0.45

The 0.45 threshold produced the highest F1 score among the tested thresholds: F1 = 85.91% compared with 85.74% at the default 0.50 threshold.

This threshold was therefore selected for the LoanIQ prediction application.

What the Model Learned

  • Total Debt-to-Income Ratio has the strongest negative coefficient, indicating that higher overall debt burden strongly reduces predicted approval probability.
  • Annual Income is one of the strongest positive contributors to approval probability.
  • Net Worth contributes positively, reflecting the importance of an applicant's financial position.
  • Loan Amount and Loan Duration have negative coefficients, indicating that larger or longer borrowing requests tend to reduce the predicted approval probability.
  • Bankruptcy History and Previous Loan Defaults negatively influence the model's prediction.
  • Longer credit history contributes positively to the prediction.

Why Logistic Regression Was Selected

Logistic Regression was selected as the final LoanIQ model because it combines strong predictive performance with high interpretability.

The tree-based models achieved very similar performance, but Logistic Regression provides coefficients that can be directly inspected.

This is particularly useful for LoanIQ because the application is designed not only to predict approval probability but also to explain the factors influencing an individual prediction.

Interpretation Note

Logistic Regression coefficients represent statistical relationships learned by the model. They indicate how features influence the model's prediction but should not be interpreted as direct causal effects.

LoanIQ is an educational and analytical machine learning project. Its predictions should not be treated as official banking or lending decisions.
